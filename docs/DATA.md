# Data and Artifact Policy

## Included

- one synthetic geometry example authored for this repository;
- selected method-level prompt templates;
- deterministic source code;
- manuscript-level aggregate integer counts;
- expected metrics derived from those counts;
- the overview figure.

## Not included

- Geometry3K or UniGeo question text and diagrams;
- raw or archived third-party datasets;
- model request/response payloads and provider identifiers;
- per-item teacher labels, free-text notes, or annotator identities;
- restricted mappings, checkpoints, manuscript drafts, or review material;
- internal audit and authorization files.

## Why

Access to a benchmark for research does not necessarily include permission to redistribute it. This repository therefore releases only synthetic content, code, documentation, and aggregate statistics that do not expose original benchmark items.

Users who want to run a full benchmark experiment must obtain each dataset from its official source under the applicable terms, construct their own local paths, and verify that their planned use is permitted.
