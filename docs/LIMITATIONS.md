# Limitations

- The released rules are lightweight consistency checks, not a complete formal geometry proof system.
- A passing text-grounded gate does not prove pixel-level diagram consistency or theorem-level equivalence.
- Predicate and answer checks can be unavailable when structured evidence is missing.
- Model-backed behavior may vary by provider, model version, prompt rendering, decoding, and service implementation.
- Main-study candidates are nested within original problems; the 512 slots are not 512 independent source problems.
- The 428/512 strict endpoint and 428/430 accepted-subset rate answer different questions and must not be interchanged.
- Challenge60 reports deterministic text-only available checks on 57 determinate labels; it is not full-verifier performance.
- Aggregate-count reproduction verifies arithmetic and reporting consistency, not end-to-end regeneration from restricted raw inputs.
- The manuscript is under review. Names, citation metadata, and release scope may change before formal publication.
